About The Competition

A unique frontend development competition that tests your debugging skills across real-world web scenarios.

🎯

Our Mission

To challenge participants to think critically, debug effectively, and demonstrate real-world frontend development skills beyond just writing code.

🔍

What Makes Us Different

Unlike typical coding contests, DECODE THE WEB focuses on investigation and debugging — skills every professional developer needs daily.

🧠

Skills Tested

HTML structure, CSS layouts, JavaScript logic, browser DevTools proficiency, debugging methodology, and attention to detail.

🏗️

Real-World Scenarios

Each challenge is based on realistic website problems — broken forms, misaligned layouts, buggy shopping carts, and more.

📊

Progressive Difficulty

From easy fixes to complex investigations, the challenges scale in difficulty so every participant can contribute and learn.

🤝

Fair Competition

All submissions are manually evaluated by experienced organizers, ensuring fair and thorough assessment of every participant's work.
